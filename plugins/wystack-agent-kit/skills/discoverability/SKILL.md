# Discoverability

Start from the product, audience, and discovery surface that matters. Verify current external rules and platform behavior with primary sources before relying on remembered guidance.

Diagnose the limiting layer, then prioritize changes by expected reach, confidence, effort, and measurability. Distinguish technical eligibility from content relevance and distribution; fixing one does not prove the others.

Prefer changes that make the product easier for both people and machines to understand. Tie recommendations to an observable query, crawl, citation, listing, or conversion signal.

Complete when the highest-leverage constraint is addressed or clearly identified, the verification method is stated, and unverified assumptions are labeled.
