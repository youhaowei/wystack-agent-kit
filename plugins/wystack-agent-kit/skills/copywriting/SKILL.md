# Copywriting

Establish the audience, desired action, offer, proof, and voice from the user's brief or project context. Preserve factual accuracy; surface unsupported claims instead of strengthening them through wording.

Lead with the user's outcome, make the value concrete, and support important claims with evidence. Match the project's voice and the reader's awareness level. Generate distinct variants for high-leverage language such as positioning, headlines, and calls to action when selection would be valuable.

Use [SEVEN-SWEEPS.md](SEVEN-SWEEPS.md) when editing existing copy and [COMPARISON-PAGES.md](COMPARISON-PAGES.md) for alternative or versus pages.

Complete when the copy is specific, credible, coherent in voice, and gives the intended reader a clear next action.
